# Sunday weekly planning workflow

Confirmed requirement: a Sunday workflow sets the week's framework, including changes to office days and proposed gym times. It precedes nightly task planning and morning adjustments. Sunday evening is confirmed; the exact clock time remains to be chosen. Planning the upcoming Monday–Sunday is a proposed date range, not yet confirmed; show explicit dates in the product.

## Intended experience

Keep the check-in short, guided, and editable. Prepare a draft before the review from authorized calendar data, the usual weekly pattern, unresolved commitments, and the latest owner feedback. Show only the changes and decisions that matter. A proposed 5–10-minute review is a design target, not an observed duration or mandatory time limit.

1. **What is different this week?** Confirm office days, start/finish times, class exceptions, appointments, travel, and any unusual commitments. Prefill the usual pattern instead of asking the owner to enter it every week. Office days can change; Tuesday/Wednesday are defaults, not hard-coded limits.
2. **Choose the week's priorities.** Surface a small set of business, assignment, personal, and household outcomes. Preserve actual deadlines and show capacity concerns. A weekly priority is not automatically a fixed calendar appointment.
3. **Approve gym times.** Propose complete blocks including preparation, both walks, and the specified cardio. Use the usual split, actual weekly availability, and owner-supplied adjustments. Show the whole week's times together for approval. Ask before changing the workout-day pattern.
4. **Approve errand outings and protect life needs.** Group compatible errands into realistic continuous outings with travel, dinner/meal preparation needs, and personal anchors visible. Keep unknown travel/logistics explicit. Do not stuff outings into short meeting gaps. Offer editable proposed blocks rather than assuming Friday is always the errand day.
5. **Review and approve the framework.** Show the dated week and the meaningful changes: office days/hours, protected events, gym blocks, errand outings, major deadlines, and available capacity. Accept all or adjust selected items. Distinguish external events, private approved blocks, and pending proposals. No repeated approval question is needed for unchanged gym/errand blocks already approved in this review.

## Relationship to daily planning

The approved framework provides boundaries for the nightly planner. It automatically places ordinary flexible tasks inside the remaining eligible time. The morning refresh adjusts that same daily plan based on changes and feedback. Neither process silently moves protected calendar events or approved gym/errand blocks.

If the owner explicitly changes an office day or another major constraint midweek, produce a concise revised framework showing affected commitments. Ask approval for revised gym/errand blocks; automatically rearrange only eligible flexible tasks. Do not wait until next Sunday to resolve a real conflict.

An approval applies to the actual proposed dates/times and scope. Background sync must not mutate a pending proposal invisibly; if its underlying calendar constraints change, mark it stale and present the updated changes before approval. Writing to external calendars requires the separately configured authorization and destination calendar.

## Missed or interrupted review

Save partially completed reviews and allow resuming. If Sunday is missed, offer catch-up; do not claim the new week was approved. Use existing fixed calendar commitments and established defaults only where their status is clear. A prior week's approved gym times do not automatically authorize copying them to a new week. Leave approval-required blocks pending while ordinary task management continues.

Handle unavailable accounts with stale-data labels, not assertions that the week is free. Keep one active review per dated week, persist approval/version history, and avoid duplicate notifications or competing plans across devices. Weekly-review reminder escalation must be configured explicitly; a missed review is not simply an ordinary task whose framework can be automatically rescheduled and approved.

## Acceptance examples

- Owner changes this week's office days from Tuesday/Wednesday to Monday/Wednesday. Draft gym/errand blocks respect the new office/travel constraints without changing the user's real meetings.
- Owner approves the weekly gym times. Nightly planning fits assignments around them without asking again or moving them silently.
- An approved gym block conflicts with a new meeting. The app shows the conflict and offers a revised block for approval.
- Owner abandons the review halfway through. Returning resumes it with saved choices; no unfinished proposal was treated as approved.
- A new calendar event arrives while approval is pending. The app refreshes the affected proposal and shows the change before approval.
